Clawd rig

Chips for loops set the base; one-shots play once over the current base. Flows replay the mood engine's sequences (src/web/mood.js) on this page's clock, so the clip changes look as they will on the dashboard. Freeze stops the player and the slider scrubs the selected clip. Freeze a frame from the URL with ?anim=walk&t=1200&seed=2, or from the console with clawdDebug.freeze('hop', 420), clawdDebug.play('walk') and clawdDebug.flow('flow_turn').
